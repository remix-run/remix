Added `@remix-run/ui/tooltip` for visual-only hover and focus tooltips. Tooltips open after a hover delay or immediately on keyboard focus, close on blur, press, outside press, or Escape, stay open while the pointer moves onto them, and position with `anchor()`. Tooltips join the shared overlay stack, so they paint above the dialog or popover that contains their trigger and stay inside its focus boundary when portaled. `tooltip.GroupContext` shares delays so neighboring tooltips open instantly once one is showing.
