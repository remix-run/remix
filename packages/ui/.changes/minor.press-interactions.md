Add `@remix-run/ui/press`, an opt-in `press()` mixin that activates a host on primary mouse button press instead of release by calling its native click and cancelling the click that follows. Checkboxes, radios, buttons, submit and reset buttons, labels, and existing `click` listeners behave natively and run once; touch, pen, keyboard, and assistive technology keep the native click. The dispatched click is an untrusted `PointerEvent` carrying the press coordinates, button, modifiers, and `detail: 1`. Early activation trades away drag-off-to-cancel (WCAG 2.5.2 Pointer Cancellation), and `window` or `document` capture listeners such as analytics also see the trailing trusted click.

Every Remix UI primitive activates on click, as before: options, menu items, tabs, accordion triggers, toggle, `form.label()`, `dialog.trigger()`, `dialog.close()`, and popover anchors. Compose `press()` onto any of them, such as `mix={[tabs.tab({ name }), press()]}`, to activate on mouse press. `popover.anchor()`, `dialog.trigger()`, and `dialog.close()` keep a mouse press from moving focus to the button, so a press-activated trigger does not take focus from the overlay it opens.

Select and menu triggers open on primary mouse button press, and a held press released over an option or menu item commits it. In an open select or menu, a press that starts on one item and is released over another commits the item under the release, and releasing outside the list does nothing. Items commit on release, never on press. Touch, pen, and keyboard are unchanged.

`press()` also reports the press lifecycle, covering the use cases of the former `pressEvents` mixin. Pass `onPressStart`, `onPressEnd`, and `onLongPress` (with `longPressDuration`, 500ms by default) to follow primary-button mouse, touch, and pen presses and Enter and Space presses, including coordinates, pointer type, and cancellation by moving off, scrolling, blur, or Escape. Pass `activate: false` for feedback only, such as a pressed style or ripple, so activation stays on the native click. `onLongPress` defers activation and consumes a long press, so nothing is activated: the mouse activates on click, and Enter on a native `button`, link, `summary`, or button-like `input` activates when the key is released, by clicking the host, so a submit button still submits as the submitter and a link still navigates. Hosts without `onLongPress` keep the browser's own Enter. The click from an Enter release is untrusted and relies on the keydown's transient user activation, which browsers keep for about five seconds.

Outside dismissal ignores the click of a press that started inside the layer or before it opened. Closing a popover no longer pulls focus back to its trigger when another element has already taken focus.

Listbox options and menu items, including disabled ones, now set `cursor: default` and `user-select: none` (with `-webkit-user-select`) so their text shows no text cursor and can't be selected. Listbox options highlight on `pointermove` (mouse and pen) instead of `mousemove`, so the highlight follows the pointer while a press that started on the select trigger is still held. `press()` hosts and `form.label()` labels set the same styles, and text-entry controls on or inside them keep their native cursor and selection.
