Disable automatic focus resets during intercepted navigation with `data-rmx-reset-focus="false"` on links, forms, or submit buttons, or with `resetFocus: false` in `navigate()` and `link()`. This selects the Navigation API's `focusReset: "manual"` behavior and carries through history replacements, redirects, and back/forward navigation. By default, the browser retains its `"after-transition"` focus behavior.
