Fix `toggle`, `select`, and `combobox` falling out of sync when their form is reset. An uncontrolled native checkbox `toggle` now restores `defaultChecked`, including `data-state`, and `select` and `combobox` restore `defaultValue`. A controlled `toggle` keeps its `checked` value. Like a native reset, none of them call change handlers or dispatch change events. Fix `toggle()` throwing when called without options.
