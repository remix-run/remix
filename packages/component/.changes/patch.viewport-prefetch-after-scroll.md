Links with `data-rmx-prefetch="viewport"` now start prefetching during browser idle time. Links that enter the viewport while the page or one of its scrollers is scrolling also wait until scrolling has stopped for 150ms, and are prefetched only if they're still in view. A fast scroll through a long list no longer prefetches, and has the server render, every page it passes. Pending viewport work pauses while the document is hidden and resumes with fresh visibility and eligibility checks when it becomes visible.

Default prefetching also waits for idle time before parsing downloaded HTML and discovering assets. Idle callbacks have a one-second timeout to keep busy pages making progress, and browsers without idle callbacks yield through timers. Intent and imperative triggers keep their existing timing. Pending viewport work and HTML discovery are cancelled when the runtime is disposed.
