Links with `data-rmx-prefetch="viewport"` that enter the viewport while the page or one of its scrollers is scrolling now wait until scrolling has stopped for 150ms, and are prefetched only if they're still in view. A fast scroll through a long list no longer prefetches, and has the server render, every page it passes. Links the visitor stops on are prefetched as before, and links that enter the viewport without a scroll, such as on page load, still prefetch right away.
