Fix back and forward navigations returning to the wrong scroll position. In Safari, a traversal whose frames took longer than 300 ms to load restored the position of the page being left, or the top when the visitor went back right after a navigation, because WebKit saves the window's position into the destination's history entry while it loads. In Firefox, a destination taller than the page being left came back clamped to that page's height whenever it took longer than a frame to load. In Chromium, a wheel scroll just before going back ended at the top. Remix now returns the window to where the visitor left the destination once the browser has restored, unless the visitor scrolls during the navigation or the destination restores scroll manually with `history.scrollRestoration = 'manual'`.

Interrupting a back or forward navigation before its page renders also preserves that page's saved scroll position for the next visit. If a direct reload of the target frame or any ancestor takes over the navigation and renders the page, subsequent visits restore the position the visitor left after that reload.
