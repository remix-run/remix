Pass an anchor to `prefetch()` to use its navigation source and frame target and expose it to custom hooks. These prefetches cancel when the link disconnects or its destination or eligibility changes. Removing or disabling `data-rmx-prefetch` also cancels declarative work. See [Prefetching](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/blob/main/packages/component/README.md#prefetching) for usage.
