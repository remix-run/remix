Follow a reload through `reloadStart`, now a `FrameReloadStartEvent` describing its effective request with `src`, lowercase `method`, `encType`, `formData`, `element`, and `submitter`. These align with `frame.reload()` options, with `body` exposed as `formData`. GET entries are included in `src`, while `formData` and `encType` are null. Descendant frames inherit the initiating request details and track their own completion.

The event's `finished` promise always resolves with a `FrameReloadOutcome` containing `{ error, aborted }`. Success has no error and `aborted: false`; cancellation, replacement, or removal sets `aborted: true` with no error. Failures carry an `Error`, preserving other thrown values as its `cause`. The promise returned by `frame.reload()` still rejects on failure. See [following a reload](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/tree/main/packages/component#following-a-reload) for the full contract.
