- Improved request and response handling throughput in `createRequestListener()`, `createRequest()`, and `sendResponse()`. As part of this change, `sendResponse()` no longer releases its reader lock on the consumed `response.body` stream after writing.
- `createRequestListener()` now always constructs and passes a `Request` to handlers with `.length === 0` (such as rest-parameter handlers) and routes their errors through `onError` consistently with other handlers.
