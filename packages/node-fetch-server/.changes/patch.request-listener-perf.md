Improve request and response handling throughput in `createRequestListener()`, `createRequest()`, and `sendResponse()`. As part of this change, `sendResponse()` no longer releases its reader lock on the consumed `response.body` stream after writing, bodyless HTTP/1.x requests (with `Content-Length: 0` or neither `Content-Length` nor `Transfer-Encoding`) now leave `request.body` as `null` instead of wrapping an empty stream, and `createRequestListener()` now always constructs and passes a `Request` to handlers with `.length === 0` (such as rest-parameter handlers) and routes their errors through `onError` consistently with other handlers.
