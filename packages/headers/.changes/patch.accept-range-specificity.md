Fix `Accept`, `Accept-Encoding` and `Accept-Language` weights when a wildcard or broader range has a higher quality than a more specific entry. `getWeight()`, `accepts()` and `getPreferred()` now use the most specific matching range, as RFC 9110 requires. Previously the highest-weighted match won, so `Accept-Encoding: gzip;q=0, *` reported `gzip` as acceptable and `compressResponse()` would gzip a response for a client that refused it.
