`createMatcher()` and `createMultiMatcher()` match URLs much faster and use less memory, especially with large route tables. In our benchmarks, matching against 1,000 to 5,000 patterns is 20 to 60 times faster, building a matcher is 5 to 9 times faster, and patterns with many optional segments or wildcards no longer slow down sharply. Match results and specificity are unchanged.
