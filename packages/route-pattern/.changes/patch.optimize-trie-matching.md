`createMatcher()`, `createMultiMatcher()`, and `createHref()` are significantly faster and use less memory, especially with large route tables. In our benchmarks, matching against 1,000 to 5,000 patterns is 20 to 60 times faster, building a matcher is 5 to 9 times faster, `createHref()` is 1.2 to 1.9 times faster, and patterns with many optional segments, wildcards, or `matchAll()` candidates no longer slow down sharply. Match results, specificity, and generated hrefs are unchanged.
