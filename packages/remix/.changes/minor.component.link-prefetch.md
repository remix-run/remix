Add `data-rmx-prefetch="intent"` or `data-rmx-prefetch="viewport"` to links, or call `prefetch(href)` from `remix/component`, to start loading pages before a visitor opens them. Intent links prefetch on hover, focus, or press; viewport links prefetch when they enter view, including links added during frame navigation or client rendering. Remix fetches HTML to discover JavaScript, CSS, and explicit image and font preloads for asset prefetch hints, then leaves navigation and response caching to the browser. Even uncacheable HTML can reveal cacheable assets. `run({ prefetch })` lets apps replace this strategy (see #12004).
