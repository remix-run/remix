`remix/auth` supports passwordless email sign-in with magic links and one-time email codes. Create a provider with `createMagicLinkAuthProvider()` or `createEmailOTPAuthProvider()`, send credentials with `sendMagicLink()` or `sendEmailOTP()`, and check them with `verifyMagicLink()` or `verifyEmailOTP()` before calling `completeAuth()`. Applications provide the storage, email delivery, and user lookup. See the [`auth` changelog](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/blob/main/packages/auth/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
