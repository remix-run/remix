`remix/auth` now supports passkeys (see #11965). Server routes register and verify passkeys with `createPasskeyAuthProvider()` and the `startPasskeyRegistration()`, `finishPasskeyRegistration()`, `startPasskeyAuthentication()`, and `finishPasskeyAuthentication()` helpers, and the new `remix/auth/browser` entrypoint runs the browser's passkey prompts and autofill with `createPasskey()` and `getPasskey()`. See the [`auth` changelog](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/blob/main/packages/auth/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
