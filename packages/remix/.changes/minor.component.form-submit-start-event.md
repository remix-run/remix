Follow a form's own frame submissions with `onSubmission()` from `remix/component`. Its noncancelable `FormSubmissionEvent` exposes `form: HTMLFormElement` and shares the request details and `finished` promise with the target frame's `reloadStart`. Its `finished` promise follows `frame.reload()`: it resolves with an `AbortSignal` and rejects on failure. Cancellation resolves with an aborted signal.
