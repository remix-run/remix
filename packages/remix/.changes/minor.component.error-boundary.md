Render fallback content for parts of a page that fail to render with `<ErrorBoundary>` from `remix/component`. On the server, the rest of the document still renders with your response status, and the caught error goes to `onError`. In the browser, a boundary catches render errors in its children and can retry them. See the [`component` changelog](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/blob/main/packages/component/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
