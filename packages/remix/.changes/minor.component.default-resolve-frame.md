Customize frame fetching and rendering with `defaultResolveFrame` from `remix/component`. Its `{ fetch, commit }` overrides let apps manage cached responses and animate navigations, including followed redirects. Resolvers receive the original `navigationType` and `from` history entry; `commit` receives the resolved destination, and `commitFrame()` waits for rendering and navigation completion. See the bookstore demo for [traversal caching](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/tree/main/demos/bookstore#frames) and [shared cover/title animations](https://github.com/remix-run/remix/tree/main/demos/bookstore#ui).
