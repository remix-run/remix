`compressResponse()`, and with it the `compression()` middleware, now compresses Brotli at quality 5 instead of Node's default of 11. Quality 11 is meant for compressing files once ahead of time, and on each response it could take longer than generating the response: tens of milliseconds for a 50 KB HTML page and hundreds for a large one. Quality 5 takes under a millisecond for the same page, and its output is still smaller than gzip's. Set `brotli.params[zlib.constants.BROTLI_PARAM_QUALITY]` to use another quality.
